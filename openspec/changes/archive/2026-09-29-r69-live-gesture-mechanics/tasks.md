## 2. The gesture waits for the read input

- [x] 2.1 `click`, `right_click`, `key`, `wheel`, `hover`: a `seen()` mark before the gesture, `wait_seen` after it instead of a fixed pause; the `soft` outcome is a NAMED pause that falls within the budget. Check: t807 (`reach_tests.rs`) plus the live `rmb.py`, `keys.py`, `level.py` — the verdicts are the same. **Done.** A common `_after_gesture(mark, what)`: waits for the counter of read input to grow, the deadline is short (`max(2 s, 12 frame steps)`), the fallback is a NAMED pause of 0.25 s within the budget. `hover` additionally waits for quiet: its consequence is a picture, and `analysis` compares the SCREEN (without this, 2.9 % instead of 0.2 %).
- [x] 2.2 `drag`: a frame wait between positions instead of `pause(0.12)`, a wait for read input after the press and after the release; the gap "the press survives a frame" remains a frame wait, not a clock wait. Check: live `select.py`, `corner.py`, `level.py`, `geomctl.py` (fine gestures with a 3 px tolerance) — the verdicts are the same. **Done.** Each step of the drag waits for the read movement, "the press survives a frame" and "the last position has reached a frame" — `wait_frame`. **The batch after the release is `wait_frame(2)`, NOT `settle()`:** the measurement showed that quiet after the gesture never arrives at all (the program is legitimately redrawing under the pointer), and `settle` burned its timeout — 10 s on EACH drag, +300 s per run. For the same reason the deadline is limited for `hover` and `close_window` (`analysis` — 54 s instead of 17 s).
- [x] 2.3 `double_click`: the 0.04 s gap between clicks REMAINS a pause with a reason (the temporal gesture of egui, the R.34 measurement: 5 of 5 against 0 of 5), and the wait after it goes by read input. Check: live `fxwin.py`, `audiofx.py`, `formula.py` — the effect window opens. **Done.** The 0.04 s gap is kept with its previous reason; hover and the double click itself wait for read input.
- [x] 2.4 `type_text` sends the line whole and waits ONCE (the decision of R.68, `design.md` item 2, not implemented). Check: live `formula.py` (typing `sin(t)*3+5` reaches the file) and `nestparam.py`. **Done.** The line goes in a batch for one wait; a character typed WITH SHIFT (`*`, `+`, `(`) is taken out of the batch and goes in its own frame — otherwise the trouble of R.63 would repeat ("`*` comes out as an 8"), and now also because the modifier needs its own frame.
- [x] 2.5 Replace the unconditional `focus()` in the gestures with `raise_once()`; reset the flag on entering the stand, for its own process, and after `resize()`. Check: t807 — two gestures in a row in one window raise it once; live `forms.py` (a low window) and `recover.py` (its own process). **Done.** The gestures call `raise_once()`; along the way it was found that `_raised` was NEVER set to `True` — `raise_once` raised the window every time, that is, the mechanism of R.68 was dead.
- [x] 2.6 The calls `probe.move/button/key` from the harness pass their own pause (almost zero); the defaults of `ui-probe.py` for a manual run are not changed. Check: the guard from 1.2 sees the new number, and a manual `python3 scripts/ui-probe.py` still works on its own screen. **Done.** The harness passes `pause=0.0` to the driver; the defaults of a manual run are untouched.
